\newcommand{\biaspage}[4]{%
  \begin{center}\Large #1: #2\end{center}
  \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figs/record_length_aggregate_bias_#3.pdf}
  \par\medskip
  Each run first gives one overall error across the six parameters:
  \[
    E_N^{(r)}=\|\widehat{\boldsymbol\theta}_N^{(r)}-
                         \boldsymbol\theta^\star\|_2
      =\left[\sum_{j=1}^{6}
        (\widehat\theta_{j,N}^{(r)}-\theta_j^\star)^2\right]^{1/2}.
  \]
  The plotted means and pointwise approximate $95\%$ Monte Carlo
  confidence intervals across retained runs are
  \[
    \overline E_N=\frac{1}{R_N}\sum_{r\in\mathcal I_N}E_N^{(r)},
    \qquad
    \overline E_N\pm1.96\,\frac{s_{E,N}}{\sqrt{R_N}},
  \]
  where $s_{E,N}$ is the sample standard deviation of the per-run errors.
  These intervals describe uncertainty in the mean overall error;
  they are not intervals for an individual run or simultaneous bands.
  All six parameters enter on their original scale, with equal weights.
  Finite stalled and iteration-limited outputs are retained; failed fits
  are excluded. Linear vertical axes start at zero and are capped at one,
  with tighter ranges retained. Points and interval portions outside the
  display range are clipped; boundary triangles mark clipped means.
  #4
  \clearpage}
